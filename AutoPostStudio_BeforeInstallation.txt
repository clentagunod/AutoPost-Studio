WELCOME TO AUTOPOST STUDIO

Thank you for installing AutoPost Studio.

AutoPost Studio is designed to help you prepare, schedule, and automate social-media publishing from one application.

BEFORE YOU CONTINUE

• Make sure you have permission to use every social-media account you connect.
• Keep your account credentials and API access tokens private.
• Check the publishing rules and API policies of each connected platform.
• Review your scheduled posts before enabling automatic publishing.
• An internet connection may be required for connected-platform features.

IMPORTANT

Third-party platforms can change their APIs, permissions, rate limits, or publishing requirements. A successful setup inside AutoPost Studio does not guarantee that a third-party platform will publish every post.

If you are installing AutoPost Studio for the first time, we recommend completing the account/API connection setup after installation and testing with a single post before enabling a larger automation schedule.
